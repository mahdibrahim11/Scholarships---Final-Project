How it works

  1. Pick your state, grade, and field.
    Scholarships open to everyone in the country show up alongside the ones for your state.
  2. Compare awards and deadlines.
    Each result shows the amount, the due date, and what you need to apply, such as an essay or a minimum GPA.
  3. Apply on the official page.
    We link to the provider's own site and show when we last checked the details.
